Divine Intimacy Temple
Cacao and Cuddles

Sunday, Nov 8th | 16:45 – 23:30

Step into a sacred space where presence, connection, and conscious intimacy meet. Together, we co-create a container of safety, play, and raw authentic expression — a place to reconnect with the bliss within ourselves, others, and our community.

✨ What is a Temple?

A Temple is a conscious, sacred space that honors the divine spark in each of us. Here we merge conscious awareness of ourselves and others with clear, respectful communication about boundaries, consent, and negotiation together with our erotic, sensual, and primal energies.

At its core, the Temple is about developing a conscious, safe, loving, intimate, and sensual connection with yourself first. From this grounded place, we may then choose to experience authentic connection with others.

🌍 Who is this Temple for?

For beginners and experienced practitioners alike — singles or couples, all identities and orientations. Whether this is your first Temple or you have attended many before, the invitation is the same: come with openness, curiosity, and respect.

⚠️ Important Safety Agreement: There is no sex in the Temple. No physical penetration of any kind. Nudity is optional. Every practice is an invitation, never an expectation.

✨ How will the event look?

First, through guided movement, dance, breathwork, asanas, meditation, rituals, and sharing circles, we connect to our emotions, desires, and life-force energy. These practices help us:

  • Become more aware of our bodies
  • Emotionally release and regulate the nervous system
  • Create meaningful containers of connection
  • Express boundaries and desires clearly
  • Practice consensual relating and clean communication

Finally, we move into the Open Space — the unguided free-flow portion of the Temple — where participants are invited to bring what they have learned into conscious intimate interactions and heart-centred experiences with their partner or others in the room.

Held in an open, shared space. Conducted in English with Polish translation.
