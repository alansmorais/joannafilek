How Can I Help You?

I work primarily with individuals facing emotional crises, daily life challenges, and relationship difficulties.

Emotional Crises & Distress

Depression, anxiety disorders, chronic stress, burnout, grief, and significant life transitions.

Relationships & Intimacy

Communication barriers, relationship crises, feelings of loneliness, and fostering healthy attachments.

Self-Esteem & Boundaries

Low self-worth, difficulty saying no, quieting the harsh inner critic, and practicing self-acceptance.

Personal Growth

Deepening self-understanding, improving emotional regulation, and cultivating psychological resilience.

Sexual Health & Intimacy

Sexual challenges rooted in emotional causes (performance anxiety, shame, inhibitions, and blockages).

Navigating Life Changes

Major life decisions, career pivots, relocation, and searching for authentic purpose and direction.

What Does the First Consultation Look Like?

Our initial meeting is a calm, unhurried conversation about your circumstances, challenges, and what you need right now.

1.

Conversation Without Pressure

Share only as much as you feel ready to share. Nothing is forced, and everything unfolds at your own pace.

2.

Identifying Your Needs

Together, we clarify what issues you wish to address and what type of therapeutic support serves you best.

3.

Creating a Safe Sanctuary

We work in complete discretion, free from judgment, shame, or artificial time pressure.

4.

Formulating a Path Forward

Together, we craft an individualized therapy plan to help you regain clarity, vitality, and emotional balance.

Psychotherapy and Sexological Consultations – FAQ

What does private psychotherapy look like?

A private psychotherapy practice offers a discreet, confidential sanctuary for individuals facing life crises, relationship struggles, or burnout. Professional psychological guidance helps identify the root causes of distress and develop resilient coping strategies for daily life.

When is it helpful to seek a sexological consultation?

Difficulties around intimacy, desire, and communication are common. A sexological consultation offers a safe, professional space free from shame to address inhibitions, performance anxiety, and sexual health questions for both individuals and couples.

Is couples therapy effective during a relationship crisis?

Yes, couples therapy helps rebuild mutual trust and break destructive conflict loops. Working together with a therapist provides structured communication tools to understand underlying emotional needs and restore connection.
