First, through guided movement, dance, breath work, asanas, meditation, rituals and sharing circles we will connect to our emotions, desires and life force energy.

Held in English with Polish translation.

“The act of transgression breaks through the barriers of fear and shame, and that moment of liberation creates a charge of energy on which we can ride to heights of pleasure and ecstatic experience. This Temple is a deep transformational workshop using sacred power, polarity, and conscious play as a pathway into freedom, intimacy, and authentic power.

  • Desire to heal shame or old conditioning around sexuality, power, and vulnerability

  • Value consent, communication, and emotional responsibility
  • Are curious about inner work, growth, and self-reflection
